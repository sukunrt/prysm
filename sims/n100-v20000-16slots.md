# 100-node comparison extended to 16 slots

2026-10-06. Both fresh Shadow runs completed successfully on ethp2p after
launching in parallel. Prysm took 1,935.69 seconds and eth-slot-sim 725.91 seconds.
Final-slot, connectivity and across-slot analyses completed and were collected locally.

The configuration matches the previous logging rerun: 100 nodes, 20,000
validators, 80 home and 20 super nodes; one FFG subnet and one 2,500-validator
committee per slot; eight staggered voter groups repeating over 16 slots; ePBS;
individual FFG voting at slot start with zero configured jitter/spread; FFG
aggregates at 9s and PTC deadline at 6s; approximately 256 KiB execution payloads
and a 200,000,000 gas limit. The Spamoor workload and its seed are preserved.
Actual included payload and blob sizes will be checked per slot.

Both CL and EL are configured for 99 participant peers per node. Runtime audits
check identities before and after Prysm's measured slots and at every
eth-slot-sim slot boundary. Peer scoring, transport and publication behavior are
unchanged. Binaries match the checksums used in the previous comparison, including
the unconditional ignored-aggregate logging and current queue sizes.

The driver produced final-slot results (Prysm slot 16 /
eth-slot-sim slot 15), receipt/connectivity audits, and individual FFG statistics
across all 16 slots. Reports include median, average, p95 and p99, broken down by
home/super receiver class and node. A plot compares class average/p95/p99 across
slots. The analysis also matches included blob transaction hashes to EL RPC
submission logs to examine the correlation found in the eight-slot run.

Both final-slot FFG ledgers contain all 247,500 expected remote receipts.
All 100 nodes agree on the final Prysm head and payload. Runtime identity audits
confirm 99 CL and EL peers per Prysm node before/after the run, and all 1,700
eth-slot-sim node/boundary audits show 99 peers. No final-slot errors or parser
failures were logged.

Prysm's final execution payload is **233,593 bytes**, versus eth-slot-sim's
262,144 bytes (10.89% smaller). It contains 16 transactions and six blobs; gas
limit is 200,000,000 and gas used 15,004,064. The beacon block contains one
FFG aggregate and one payload attestation. All measured blocks retain the
200-million gas limit. The payload misses the diagnostic ±10% size band.

The final slot publishes 21 Prysm FFG aggregates at 9,000–9,001ms, each with
2,500 seats. All 1,218 observed remote aggregate records are ignored as already
known; these are observed receipts, not complete delivery to every receiver.

Availability completeness is false: 1 nodes have fewer than 512 accepted/local/replayed seats;
31 availability records were dropped as not_current_slot. See the raw report
for the per-node counts; the individual FFG ledger is complete.
